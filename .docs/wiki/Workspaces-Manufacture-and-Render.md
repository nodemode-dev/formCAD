# Manufacture, render and costing

**Manufacture.** Setups, tools, operations (`camsetup`, `camtool`, `camop`, `camgenerate`), 5-axis and stock simulation, turning (`camturnprofile`), probing (`camprobe`), cutting and nesting (`camnest`), posts for LinuxCNC and grbl (`camexport`). Always simulate and inspect NC output before cutting.

**Print.** FormCAD's own slicer: `slice`, `orientstudy`, `arrangeplate`, `additiveexport`. Marlin G-code, supports, a layer preview.

**Render and animation.** Path-traced rendering, environments and appearances that name their sources, final frames up to 4K, storyboards and MP4 video. No GPU timings are claimed.

**Costing.** A profile of your own rates; per-part estimates; design-for-manufacture findings with editable thresholds; emissions from factors you import. Nothing is pre-filled.

See manual chapters 15, 16 and 23.
